How to Play:

  • There is a new professional match every day for each region (LCK, LPL, LEC, LCS) and for international events (Worlds, MSI, First Stand), plus a Worlds only game.
  • Matches come from tier 1 league playoffs, MSIs, and Worlds from 2013-present.
  • You get 5 attempts per game. First guess the year: green is correct, the arrow shows if the answer is higher or lower
  • Next pick the event from that year, e.g. OGN Winter, Spring or Summer Playoffs, or MSI vs Worlds (skipped on the Worlds tab)
  • Then guess the blue side team, and once that's right, the red side team. Every wrong guess uses an attempt. Green means the right team, yellow means that team played but on the other side, red means wrong team
  • From 2017 on, picks are shown in the order they were drafted (B1 = blue side's first pick). Use the Draft order / Roles switch to line them up by lane instead
  • After 3 wrong guesses you can use one hint button to display a random player from that team. Another wrong guess unlocks the other hint

Have fun
